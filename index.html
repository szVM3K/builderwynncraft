About the Build Recommender

Builder Wynncraft picks a complete Wynncraft build for you: helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots, two rings, a bracelet, a necklace and a weapon for your class. Choose your class, rank and level, an ability tree (an archetype, a guide tree or your own), what to maximise - a spell, the main attack or a whole spell cycle - and the effective HP you need. The recommender searches all 5,414 items for the strongest build that you can actually wear with your skill points and that meets your survivability, mana and walk speed limits.

Every number follows Wynnbuilder's formulas and item data for game version 2.2.4 and is checked against Wynnbuilder: spell and main attack damage with crits averaged in, effective HP from Defence, Agility and your tree, mana and life balance of your spell cycle, and the Major IDs the model can simulate. The Build Optimizer keeps the items you already chose and fills the rest; the Build Creator is a manual editor with the same live numbers and Wynnbuilder import and export.
