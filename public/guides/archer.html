Archer builds: archetypes, skills and guide builds

The Archer attacks from range with a bow. Base spells: Arrow Storm, Escape, Arrow Bomb and Arrow Shield. Sharpshooter replaces Arrow Storm with Phantom Ray, and Boltslinger's fourth spell becomes Guardian Angels. The class factor in the EHP model is 1.3, so Archers need noticeably more Health or Defence and Agility than Warriors for the same EHP - one reason Agility is popular on Archer builds.

Boltslinger

Boltslinger spams Arrow Storm and lets the ability tree supply mana and speed. It is a thunder archetype at heart, so Dexterity and Strength are the usual skill investments, and it values Spell Damage (raw and %) above everything else.

  • Usual skill points: Strength, Dexterity
  • Usual elements: Thunder
  • Stats the site's archetype profile weighs highest (used for Other picks and the Build Solver): Spell Damage (raw), Spell Damage %, Water Damage (raw), Damage %, Thunder Damage %

Sharpshooter

Sharpshooter stacks Focus into big hits: Phantom Ray, Crepuscular Ray, Twain's Arc and The Twilight Zone come from its tree. Spell Damage %, Damage % and Critical Damage are its top stats. Every element appears somewhere among its guide builds.

  • Usual skill points: Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Agility
  • Usual elements: Air, Earth, Water, Fire, Thunder
  • Stats the site's archetype profile weighs highest (used for Other picks and the Build Solver): Spell Damage %, Damage %, Critical Damage %, Spell Damage (raw), Elemental Damage %
  • Abilities the suggested tree adds to the damage panel: Twain's Arc, Crepuscular Ray, The Twilight Zone, Warding Wisps

Trapper

Trapper uses traps and pets - Basaltic Trap, Call of the Hound, Murder Flock, Chilling Snare - with earth and air damage. The guide rates it as weak for now, and it has the fewest guide builds, so treat generated Trapper builds as experiments.

  • Usual skill points: Strength, Agility
  • Usual elements: Earth, Air
  • Stats the site's archetype profile weighs highest (used for Other picks and the Build Solver): Fire Damage %, Spell Damage %, Strength, Mana Regen, Earth Damage %
  • Abilities the suggested tree adds to the damage panel: Basaltic Trap, Call of the Hound, Murder Flock, Chilling Snare

Generating a Archer build

  1. Open the Build Recommender, pick Archer, your rank and level.
  2. Choose an archetype, or one of the guide trees if you want a known endgame tree. The spells in the "Maximise" list come from that tree.
  3. Pick what to maximise and set your effective HP minimum. Class factor 1.3 means EHP numbers aren't directly comparable with other classes.
  4. Type your spell cycle if you want a sustainable build, then press Generate.
  5. Compare the result with the guide builds above: open both in Wynnbuilder, or copy the result into the Build Creator to tweak it by hand.

Guide builds come from The Ultimate Build Guide (by Sugo, maintained by helmp and gardenguy555), credited to the players who made them; "Made for" lists the content the guide recommends them for. The archetype profiles are the site's own, calibrated on those builds; the recommender itself scores items from your chosen goal on your tree.
